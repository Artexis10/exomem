# Progressive skill loading

## Why

The native Exomem skill loads about 90 KB of instructions and requests the entire
product tool catalogue before even a simple lookup. Conditional media, adoption,
Planning/Records and maintenance procedures obscure the common recall/write loop
and are paid for repeatedly by delegated agents.

## What Changes

- Keep common routing, source/write boundaries, live engagement discovery, the
  delegation envelope, and the canonical semantic authoring projection in SKILL.md.
- Route detailed procedures into bundled references with explicit read conditions.
- Discover only the tools needed for the current action; retain adapter capability
  checks and portable bootstrap fallback when local skill resources are absent.
- Regenerate the Claude plugin from the public scaffold; verify native installs,
  standalone workflows and upload archives preserve their operating contracts.

## Impact

The public scaffold and generated plugin change. Runtime tools, permissions,
semantic grammar and hosted engagement defaults do not change. Standalone
workflows gain a small shared bootstrap/retry contract and make first-write
source/link ordering explicit; their semantic grammar remains unchanged. No server-side model, optional compute capability, or live vault
migration is introduced. The reduction is an input-size measurement, not a claim
of equal long-term task outcomes or proportional invoice savings.
