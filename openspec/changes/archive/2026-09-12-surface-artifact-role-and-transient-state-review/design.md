## Context

See `proposal.md` for the problem. The current lexical `structure_promotion.py` detector groups off-scope tags; its audit counterpart uses stored vector geometry. Both can miss a change of artifact role inside one topic. Role-first category authoring already exists and remains authoritative. Collection claims now route observed state; this change does not repeat that mechanism.

`SemanticUnit` exposes category, kind/form, body/content, context, tags, relations, anchors and fingerprints. It has no general per-unit lifecycle status or creation timestamp. Corpus state already identifies eligible compiled pages and resolves references. `due_state.apply_write_delta` handles page-local findings after writes; reconcile owns whole-corpus recovery. These are the integration seams.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** recognize a small, testable set of authored role and current-state cues; retain exact evidence identity across unrelated edits; resolve by current readable state; expose bounded findings through existing carriers.

**Non-Goals:** general semantic contradiction detection; guessing conversation state; observing tool-free MCP client turns; automatically moving, deleting, superseding or rewriting content; changing retrieval rank; inventing `recipe` as a governed page kind or prescribing folders; expanding collection creation authority; absorbing the separate capture-sweep or prominence changes.

## Decisions

### D1. Two pure detectors over existing parsed units

Add `artifact_role_review.py` with no I/O or model dependency. Its inputs are immutable page/unit views and already-resolved support views. Its outputs are candidates and bounded coverage metadata. Audit and delta adapters call the same predicates. Extending topic thresholds would retain the blind spot; a second semantic model is unnecessary for this bounded first version.

Initial detection is limited to eligible compiled experiment pages. A non-experiment reusable destination may live anywhere the existing compiled eligibility contract permits, including a custom Notes subdirectory with a supported compiled type. An untyped legacy page or unsupported custom kind does not become eligible merely because its path says Recipes. The agent can inspect such a destination explicitly and use the existing governed fallback. No eligibility predicate or schema vocabulary changes here.

Use NFKC and casefold normalization local to this detector. Version-one cue tables are English and marked PROVISIONAL; do not claim universal language coverage. Unknown categories remain valid. Neither keyword count nor document length alone establishes a finding.

### D2. Artifact-role candidates need an authored boundary

`artifact_role_promotion` has two role values: `reusable_method` and `research_synthesis`.

For `reusable_method`, require (a) an authored procedure/technique unit containing an explicit reusable-method cue, and (b) a distinct reported-success result/outcome unit tied to that method. Both must be original compiled assertions, not quoted/imported instructions or tentative plans. Outcome polarity must be explicitly positive; negated, hypothetical and failed outcomes remain quiet. A successful experiment without a reusable-method cue is insufficient. Bind by an explicit exact same-method unit reference, or matching nonempty authored context containing exactly one compatible procedure and one compatible result. Two procedures sharing `batch 1` cannot borrow the same success assertion; without disambiguating unit references that group stays quiet. Use shared tags only as supporting evidence, never as the sole join. Distinct method groups produce distinct findings even on the same topic.

For `research_synthesis`, require an authored finding/inference/synthesis unit that explicitly generalizes across at least two distinct resolved source documents. Count canonical source identities, not aliases or two anchors in one source. A source list, quotation, procedure citation or single-source summary does not qualify. Only sources visible to the requesting audience contribute to the threshold.

Pin the initial cue table and exclusions in one module and paired tests. Procedure forms are rich `procedure` units or categories `procedure`/`technique`; results are rich `result` units or categories `result`/`outcome`; synthesis categories are `finding`/`inference`/`synthesis`. Initial reusable phrases are `reusable`, `repeatable`, `use this method` and `use this procedure`; positive result phrases are `worked well`, `succeeded`, `successful outcome` and `repeatable success`; synthesis phrases are `taken together`, `across these sources`, `across the studies` and `both sources`. Match whole normalized tokens, not substrings. Exclude quote/fenced-only units and clauses marked hypothetical, planned or negated; the initial exclusion tokens are `if`, `might`, `may`, `planned`, `will`, `not`, `never`, `failed` and `failure` within the qualifying clause. These are a deliberately narrow supported grammar, not an assertion that all prose fitting the concept is recognized. Any expansion requires a discriminating positive/negative pair.

Reason codes name the measured boundary, not a conclusion that the content must move. Evidence contains stable references to the qualifying units; each candidate has at most eight evidence refs and two role-appropriate action hints (`review_destination`, `extract_and_link`). There is no numeric confidence. The agent obtains full evidence with existing reads when necessary.

### D3. Promotion resolves by represented material, not shared topic

A candidate is covered only by an active, audience-readable eligible compiled destination other than the origin. A method destination must declare a reusable role through existing category/form content and contain the represented procedure plus its outcome provenance; a synthesis destination must be a research note with the represented synthesis and its source provenance. Version one defines represented content as exact equality of parsed substantive unit text after line-ending normalization only (`CRLF`/`CR` to `LF`), excluding parser-separated identity, context and relation metadata. Preserve case, Unicode, internal indentation, punctuation, values and units; the parser's existing boundary trimming remains unchanged. NFKC/casefold is for cue matching, never substantive representation. For a method, match the procedure text and link both required procedure and outcome units; for synthesis, match the synthesis text and preserve its source-document links. Exact copying is a conservative detector rule, not a writer restriction: a paraphrased destination remains a review candidate rather than receiving an invented semantic equivalence verdict. Case-sensitive identifiers and different internal code indentation are negative equality fixtures.

All required source-unit links must resolve against the current `SemanticUnitDocument.resolve_unit` identity, including the expected material fingerprint when present. Page-level `_resolve_target` success is insufficient. Missing, stale or ambiguous units and duplicate anchors cannot establish coverage. Correct links attached to unrelated content fail the representation check. A backlink to the parent page or matching tags alone cannot suppress a candidate. Coverage of some units settles only the covered candidate group.

Keep origin units for history if wanted: creating a qualifying linked destination is enough. Removing, changing, withholding or deactivating that destination reopens any uncovered candidate. Do not treat a successful write, recorded dismissal, title similarity or a stale support record as proof of coverage. Existing topic-promotion resolution stays unchanged.

Audit builds a derived source-unit-to-destination support index from its existing corpus. Store support separately from the candidate rather than deleting the candidate globally when one audience has a covering destination. At serve time recompute candidate membership and coverage using the caller's readable source, origin and destination support. A private source cannot create a public synthesis candidate; a private destination cannot silence a public candidate. Generation or policy mismatch makes that support unknown until valid state is available.

### D4. Transient state is a narrow current-state review

`transient_state_review` pairs an authored present-tense pending assertion (for example “no result yet” or “awaiting the trial result”) with an authored observed result for the same experiment episode and subject. It is a review prompt, not a verdict that either unit is false.

Episode binding uses an explicit exact unit reference or identical nonempty authored context, with a unique compatible pending/result pair for the same outcome subject. Context equality alone does not equate distinct outcome subjects: a taste result cannot settle an awaited safety result in the same batch.

The initial pending grammar is whole-clause `no <subject> result(s)/conclusion(s) [exists/exist/is available/are available] yet` or `awaiting <subject> result(s)/conclusion(s)`, where the optional subject is one to four non-stopword tokens. A result subject comes from a result unit's title or leading `<subject> result(s)/conclusion(s):` label. Normalize only case, Unicode and whitespace; do not infer synonym equivalence. Explicit unit references can disambiguate the episode but cannot override a different subject. With an omitted pending subject, require exactly one result subject and no separately named pending outcome in that episode.

The page fallback requires experiment `n` absent or equal to one, at most one distinct identifier matching `trial|run|batch` followed by a label, at most one nonempty unit context among the pair, and exactly one compatible pending/result pair for the captured subject. Conflicting contexts, multiple identifiers, labels outside the supported grammar or multiple matches disable the fallback. It is a conservative syntactic single-trial approximation, not a claim to understand every experiment design. Historical markers (`previously`, `at that time`, `before`, `earlier`), quoted/fenced clauses, future/conditional markers and different subjects are excluded. Pin these rules with raw-unit fixtures, including taste versus safety results. Do not join by topic tags alone.

An explicit date/order in authored context can establish a later result. With no such chronology, signal only the coexistence of an unqualified current “no result yet” assertion and an already-observed result for that one episode; report `current_pending_with_result`, not “later result.” Never infer time from line order, file mtime, anchor order or projection first-seen time. A new future trial after an older result, historical or quoted wording, conditional plans and different episodes are negative controls.

Resolve when the pending wording becomes historical or is removed, when a valid governed relation explicitly supersedes the old assertion, when the result is retracted or no longer bound to the same episode, or when the page leaves the applicable active lifecycle. Preserve old facts; no new per-unit status field is introduced. An unrelated edit or elapsed time alone cannot resolve or revive a signal.

### D5. Identity, limits and freshness are part of the signal

Identity is `(family, stable origin page, role or episode partition, supporting unit identities)`. `signal_version` hashes the predicate version and normalized predicate-bearing unit content, contexts and relevant resolved provenance. It excludes whole-page hashes, incidental line numbers, runtime timestamps and unrelated units. Stable anchors are preferred; use the existing unit fingerprint/occurrence identity for unanchored units. Identical duplicates cannot multiply one finding. Material evidence changes resurface a prior exact-fingerprint decision; unrelated edits do not.

PROVISIONAL per-page bounds: 400 parsed units, 8,192 inspected characters per unit, eight evidence refs per candidate, eight candidates per family per page, and eight destination supports in an explicit result. Check input limits before classification. Exceeding the input bound records capped/unknown coverage rather than treating an inspected prefix as a complete page; deterministic bounded output retains candidates without claiming all findings were listed. Persist per-family coverage internally and expose it as `meta.coverage` (`complete`, `capped`, `unknown`) on explicit audit/review results. Keep coverage diagnostics free of hidden-support counts and private path/ref details. Existing compact due-state blocks remain `total`/`categories`/`top`: omit invalid advice there, and explicitly teach that compact omission is not proof of a clean page.

Version the derived projection schema so older rows cannot be treated as complete evidence. Reconcile rebuilds from existing parsed corpus state and retains the full lightweight source-unit/destination dependency descriptors; eight is a served-support cap, not a storage truncation. Apply audience eligibility before selecting capped support details. In particular, eight private supports cannot displace a ninth public support; if a current complete caller-visible view is unavailable, coverage is unknown rather than confidently uncovered. Validate source-document generations as well as origin/destination generations and policy state.

Add page delta maintenance for both origin edits and destination support changes. Limit synchronous dependent-origin recomposition to eight origins per written page. A shared `support_epoch` increments once when fanout exceeds that limit or dependencies cannot be updated completely; row stamps from the old epoch become unknown without walking the remainder. Recompute the bounded touched set under the new epoch and let reconcile refresh the rest. This deliberately invalidates more derived advice to keep overflow handling constant-cost. Cap exhaustion while extracting changed dependency edges uses the same epoch path. The shared store must not apply one caller's visibility as permanent global settlement.

Synchronous maintenance uses the written parsed page, existing projection and already-held corpus only. No extra vault walk, Records census, destination read, embedding or generation wait occurs after commit. Missing corpus/support means unknown coverage, not a blocking repair or a proven clean result. Ordinary bootstrap/recall serves valid projected findings under its existing deadline; recomposition may invalidate stale rows but never rebuild the corpus there. Reconcile is the healer.

### D6. Reuse review lifecycle and bounded carriers

Register both families in audit, default attention and the due-state projection, with page-delta handling. Audit remains measurement-only. Normal/quiet/off and all-state inspection follow the existing family disposition contract; item dismiss/snooze/reopen uses exact fingerprints. Triage changes presentation, never the underlying coverage result.

Existing due-state blocks carry counts and bounded refs after a write and on bootstrap/recall. No additional mutation-terminal field or tool docstring is needed. This avoids competing with `records_routing` and the separate capture-sweep carrier. Preserve terminal receipts and request-budget behavior. A missing/failed detector leaves the commit intact; advice is omitted from compact carriers and unknown coverage is visible on explicit audit/review metadata. Absence of a compact block makes no clean-coverage assertion.

Bootstrap gives one compact handling clause; detailed examples belong in the shipped review/capture skills. Read the referenced units, choose a home by role, then use existing governed edits. Extraction/restructure observes `restructure_execution` confirmation; an already-authorized local correction does not acquire a new confirmation requirement. A signal itself grants no mutation authority. Teach the agent to handle visible conversation role transitions before writing too, while stating that an MCP-only server cannot guarantee capture of a tool-free turn.

### D7. Tests distinguish a useful signal from a loud one

Pure paired tests cover every predicate and negative control. Integration tests use actual audit, page writes, attention, due-state, triage and governed extraction/correction on temporary vaults. Compare delta state to full reconciliation; test restart, policy changes, deleted/renamed destinations, stale generations, partial support, duplicate units, bound exhaustion and detector exceptions. Mutation-removal probes must show that removing a family from page delta maintenance prevents immediate updates while reconcile still heals it.

Add two generic immutable replay families in a new pending benchmark amendment after sequence four: same-topic role promotion (two reusable methods plus synthesis, with protocol-only and already-promoted twins), and transient-state hygiene (same-episode current conflict, with historical and different-trial twins). Assign the next free family/receipt numbers at implementation time; never alter the pending sequence-four receipt or ratified predecessors. Pin ordinary conversational turns without storage commands. Require a signal on an actually delivered carrier after the evidence-bearing write, followed by a correct governed edit or proposal under authority; a projection-only finding is not delivered advice. Compare pre/post snapshots and require source preservation, exact unit provenance and settlement without a fabricated dismissal. Unacknowledged families remain withheld at loading, evaluation and comparison-manifest gates. Guard-blocked or incomplete native runs are harness findings, never product scores.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Conservative authored-cue detection misses implicit or non-English transitions → report bounded coverage, test explicit controls and let the active agent interpret conversation context; tune cues only with paired fixtures.
- Broad method grouping or stale links can silence unresolved work → require exact context/reference joins, per-candidate provenance and serve-time support validation.
- Destination changes can fan out → bounded reverse-index deltas, explicit invalidation and reconcile recovery within existing connector budgets.
- A review surface can become permanently noisy → test split-by-addition, exact-fingerprint dispositions and unrelated-edit stability before delivery.

## Migration Plan

No canonical data migration or new package dependency. Build both families and the additive projection version, then reconcile using the service's existing maintenance path. Preserve review decisions under the previous fingerprints; new evidence versions resurface normally. Release and perform the standard package-only service upgrade before claiming live behavior. Tool input schemas and fingerprint must remain unchanged; no action-schema refresh is introduced by this change. Rollback disables the families or installs the earlier package; derived state remains rebuildable and canonical notes are unchanged.
