## Why

`route-lifecycle-consequences-without-nudges` (slice 1) shipped the contract and the runtime for routing lifecycle consequences: two capture classes, natural-key identity on append, the Records→Planning join, the `unreflected_outcomes` family and its carriers. Its acceptance journey proves the runtime *given the expert end-state* — the test drives the library writes itself. Nothing measures the north star the dogfood note set: a real agent, on the shipped contract, given ordinary work language with every store-bearing utterance removed, produces the same durable state the expert session produced.

The bench has three neighbours and none covers it. f26 drives the real CLI envelope with no agent and measures delivery of the due-state block. Track C drives single fresh `claude -p` sessions and measures retrieval activation. f16 measures plan-versus-record divergence over authored state. No family runs a multi-turn agent through a lifecycle episode and diffs what it wrote against what an expert would have written.

Contracts-first is the house rule: the measurement files before anyone tunes the contract against an improvised test. The family is expected partial or red on today's runtime plus the slice-1 contract. That is its finding, not a CI failure — it is the falsification target the next contract slice is built against, and it is withheld from comparative claims until founder acknowledgment exactly as sequences 1 and 2 were.

## What Changes

- File §7 amendment sequence 3: family f27 `lifecycle_routing_replay` (operational journey; agent turns executed by a real agent; deterministic assertions over the projector's `collections` section), with a pending `preregistration-amendment-receipt.v1`. Registered and withheld: no comparative run, score or claim until acknowledgment.
- Register two closed-set assertions. `lifecycle_consequence_landed_unprompted`: every consequence the corpus declares an expert would land — plan items from stated intent, records from observed outcomes, status transitions from outcomes on open items — is present in the replay's projected collections; it passes only when every tier is complete and reports the per-tier fractions as its evidence. `no_structured_write_beyond_expectation`: the false-write dual — every structured item the replay produced is in the expected set with the expected state, no collection was created, and no page was written outside the declared allowlist; it passes only when the extras set is empty and lists the extras otherwise. Neither is reported without the other; there is no aggregate across tiers or arms.
- A store-bearing-utterance gate at fixture load: the corpus refuses to load if any user turn names the store or the act of storing (the pinned vocabulary lives in the corpus module, beside the retrieve-nudge's activation regex and deliberately narrower than it). A red fixture proves the refusal.
- The replay corpus: a generic batch-production transcript — an ordered list of user turns in ordinary working language, each annotated with the consequences an expert lands after it, including tentative claims and elapsed-time remarks that land nothing — plus the seeded vault (the slice-1 Planning and Records collections, joined on title) and the expected end-state folded from the annotations.
- The journey driver: discovers the `claude` executable, runs the transcript as one multi-turn session (`--session-id` on the first turn, `--resume` after) against an isolated exomem stdio server over a copy of the seeded vault, in two arms — the hookless thin client (tool surface plus the documented custom-instructions block, prominence `maximal`) and the hooked plugin client (`--plugin-dir` with the shipped skill and hooks, prominence `balanced`) — captures every stream-json transcript and hook event, projects the vault through the `VaultProjector` (0.2.0, the `collections` section) and persists the snapshot through the evidence module. A non-zero exit, an error-subtype result, a login failure or a malformed stream is a harness fault: the arm is reported blocked, never scored.
- A run report per arm: per-tier coverage beside the extras count, the number of capture-nudge firings, the structured-write tool-use count, and a manifest pinning the CLI version, the model, the exomem version, the prominence level and the corpus digest. A development run against the current runtime is recorded in the tasks as the family's finding.
- No product code, no tool-surface change, no budget constants, no catastrophic-set additions.
