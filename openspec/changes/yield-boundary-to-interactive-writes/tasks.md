## 4. Closure

- [ ] 4.0 A rehearsal of a large import (a stage of about 69,300 notes in pages of 5,000) files every page with no livelock and no refused writer. It passes when: (a) no call is refused 30 times and the planning calls of a page after the first move their cursor; (b) no writer, the cell's filer included, is refused with `holder_overdue` or `capture_wait_exhausted` (the report counts the agent's refused calls by cause, and reads a filer row of the mutation journal, `scope` owner, as one of the two); and (c) the report counts the refused writes (`paging_refusals`) by cause and the journal's `MUTATION_BUSY` rows by command, and gives the seconds of every page (`file_seconds_by_page`), the rate of each page and the projection of the whole import to 69,300 files. At that size the scan (19.9 s for `start` in run 45941473643) and the warm walk (about 6 s projected) are inside the 30 s limit, so a refusal comes from a chunk, a `plan` call or the code after an item loop, and fails (b). No baseline on `main` exists: `main` livelocks at page 2 (run 45916071821). Missing the run's 115-minute deadline (counted from its start) does not fail 4.0: it is the throughput residual of the follow-up change (the per-item plan cost, the resumable overview), unless the time per page grows from this change's own mechanisms. The ledger spans that show it are the commit and the work after it (`derived.canonical_commit`, `derived.post_canonical`), which were flat by page in that run, against `adoption.plan_items` and `adoption.prework`, which grew with the Sources. At 10.0 files per second the import needs about 6,900 s against about 5,650 s left of the deadline, so the deadline is expected to be missed until the follow-up ships.
