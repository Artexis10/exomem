# epistemic-utility-regression (delta)

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Native lifecycle evaluation defines product improvement

Exomem's in-house programme SHALL evaluate supported source preservation, agent interpretation, governed knowledge/collection projection, conversational correction, publication, fresh-session context and downstream utility as one lifecycle. It SHALL reuse existing utility and memory-loop instruments with separately versioned new scenario/metric identities. Public memory benchmarks SHALL remain external calibration or support for specific comparative claims, not product-definition or ordinary-release prerequisites. Existing frozen corpus/scorers/thresholds and applicable benchmark registration contracts SHALL remain intact. Missing lifecycle coverage SHALL remain explicit and SHALL NOT silently block unrelated independently accepted delivery slices.

#### Scenario: A public memory score rises but ordinary corrections get worse

- **WHEN** a candidate improves an external recall benchmark while Exomem-native tasks require more corrections or lose source fidelity
- **THEN** the result reports those native regressions separately and does not classify the candidate as a product improvement from the external score alone

### Requirement: Multimodal lifecycle evidence names what actually ran

Native cases SHALL bind original artifacts, available modalities, source/interpretation identity, expected supported effects and correction history. They SHALL exercise real writers, publication and subsequent public reads/activation for the claimed storage/adapter path. Text-only, transcript-only, oracle, scripted and ordinary-agent evidence SHALL remain distinct. Synthetic cases SHALL test different failure mechanisms without leaking private dogfood content or evaluator expectations to the actor. Where semantics require judgment, graders SHALL use a declared blinded rubric calibrated against retained human assessments; deterministic outcomes SHALL be preferred when sufficient.

#### Scenario: A corrected image reading is reused next session

- **WHEN** a mixed-source case preserves an image, compiles a mistaken derived value and receives a conversational correction
- **THEN** its evidence binds the corrected row/knowledge, unchanged original, historical interpretation, current publication and fresh-session useful result
- **AND** the old extraction replay and an unrelated counterexample verify that correction neither reverts nor overgeneralizes

### Requirement: Quality includes economics and human effort

Lifecycle reports SHALL show separate downstream utility/harm, epistemic correctness, context relevance/currentness, latency, token/context overhead, monetary cost, calls/retries, background/storage/index work and human correction burden. Each measure SHALL name its phase, source, units, environment, observation window and sample denominator. Unknown, partial, estimated and failed observations SHALL remain distinct from zero. Deferred work SHALL remain attributable or explicitly unmeasured; first-response time SHALL NOT stand in for completed publication or whole-task time. Fixture-supplied correction and simulated labour SHALL NOT be represented as spontaneous measured human effort. Reports SHALL expose trade-offs rather than use one weighted aggregate or infer improvement from accuracy alone.

#### Scenario: A quick capture schedules expensive work

- **WHEN** capture acknowledges quickly but media extraction or graph publication continues beyond the observation window
- **THEN** the report includes the measured acknowledgement, observed background consumption and pending/unmeasured remainder
- **AND** it does not present the acknowledgement as total lifecycle latency or cost

#### Scenario: A subscription actor has no per-call bill

- **WHEN** the client returns usage counters but no attributable monetary charge or human correction time
- **THEN** usage is reported with its provenance and charge/time remain unknown, not zero
- **AND** simulated corrections, optional price estimates and actual observed user interventions remain separate

### Requirement: Context-activation variants under the released utility family
The utility action family SHALL admit context-activation variants as a sibling variant tuple that the
cost-metered utility runner does not include in its default scope, each variant binding one cold-start case or twin to a seeded action
world whose outcome is observable state, and SHALL admit the arms control, raw recall,
compiler, nudged recall and oracle packet as paired arms of the same episode identity.
Adding a variant SHALL NOT change the family's registration, receipt or ratified
identity, SHALL keep the existing variants' seeds and outcomes byte-identical, and
SHALL keep paid replays opt-in and bounded under the existing paid-probe rule.

#### Scenario: Existing variants are unchanged
- **WHEN** the context-activation variant tuple is added beside the existing one
- **THEN** the seeds, oracles and paired outcomes of `helpful_history`,
  `self_contained` and `stale_distractor` are byte-identical to the previous release

#### Scenario: Compiler arm is paired with its controls
- **WHEN** a context-activation variant runs
- **THEN** the control, raw-recall, nudged-recall and oracle arms run on the same
  episode identity and seed as the compiler arm, and each metric is published with its
  dual
