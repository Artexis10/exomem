## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bootstrap teaches consideration and authority as separate steps

The served agent contract and installed workflow guidance SHALL teach an ordinary-work loop: consider relevant identities and meanings; resolve existing definitions and evidence; choose reuse, enrichment, justified extension, honest generic, no-edge or defer; inspect current authority; execute only permitted canonical operations; and record the decision or receipt. Guidance SHALL describe an obligation to consider supported meaning, not an obligation to invent structure.

Bootstrap SHALL report the active vocabulary workflow version, supported families, actual adapter operations, authority-contract version and whether scoped delegation is available. Compact/session profiles SHALL expose bounded summaries and read routes rather than embed a corpus census or secrets. A runtime lacking a required operation SHALL report the limitation, not teach a nonexistent shortcut.

#### Scenario: A hookless agent receives the complete loop

- **WHEN** a client without installed skills calls compact bootstrap
- **THEN** it learns how to obtain and resolve vocabulary work, how to abstain and how to distinguish proposing from permission to commit

#### Scenario: Proactivity does not imply permission

- **WHEN** the user chooses maximal prominence with no additive grants
- **THEN** the served contract encourages relevant consideration but does not authorize delegated creation

### Requirement: Vocabulary control follows the shared effective preference policy

Bootstrap and installed vocabulary guidance SHALL consume the existing prominence and delegation policy rather than define a separate vocabulary preference store. They SHALL distinguish participation, narration or interruption, and mutation authority. Portable user-profile ownership, narration overrides, revisions and bounded refresh SHALL first be specified and implemented by the owning prominence/delegation capabilities; their present per-machine configuration and bootstrap projection SHALL NOT be advertised as supplying those extensions. Once supported, preference changes SHALL be accessible through ordinary agent interaction and reflected across clients sharing the same configured profile on its required refresh. A client unable to refresh SHALL expose that limitation rather than claim to use the latest preference.

When the shared-policy extension is available, bootstrap SHALL identify its effective revision and available preference routes; it SHALL always describe only actually supported permission-inspection and owner-confirmation routes. It SHALL distinguish a supported in-conversation confirmation adapter from a configured browser fallback or an unavailable capability. Approval-presentation preferences SHALL require a definition in the owning shared-policy contract rather than an implicit vocabulary-only setting. Skills, supported hooks and thin custom instructions SHALL load or refresh the authoritative contract instead of carrying independently editable copies of it. These settings SHALL NOT change evidence requirements, disclosure policy or authority ceilings.

#### Scenario: Active maintenance can remain quiet

- **WHEN** the user's shared policy requests maximal relevant participation with quiet narration
- **THEN** the vocabulary workflow considers supported knowledge work under current authority without turning each consideration into an unsolicited user-facing interruption
- **AND** quiet narration does not suppress required approval or make unsupported claims eligible for capture

#### Scenario: A preference change reaches another client

- **WHEN** the user changes a supported engagement preference through one connected client after the shared-policy extension is available
- **THEN** another supported client of the same configured profile receives the same effective policy on its next required refresh
- **AND** the preference change neither creates a grant nor advertises an owner-confirmation adapter that the second client lacks
