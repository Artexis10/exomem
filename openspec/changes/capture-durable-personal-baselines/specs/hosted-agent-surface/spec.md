## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shared Hosted behavior ships through one immutable v5 candidate
The Hosted release pipeline SHALL designate exactly one owner for the single `hosted-alpha-agent-v5` release carrying durable-baseline, generated-artifact adoption, recurring-entity lifecycle, and governed-curation behavior. V5 SHALL own every skill and fixture it ships, including its core skill and the canonicalised snapshot of all four generic contribution inputs, and SHALL bind one combined candidate-scoped fixture digest into compatibility, package, lock, archive, and promotion evidence. No other implementation lane may edit, render, lock, archive, promote, or roll back v5 files.

The release pipeline SHALL preserve one complete v1-v4 immutability manifest covering source bytes, generated bytes, locks, archives, definitions, fixtures, promotion records, resolved command schemas, and actual-wire identities. Historical profiles MUST NOT resolve new v5-only command arguments through mutable canonical command objects.

#### Scenario: Historical candidates reproduce unchanged
- **WHEN** v1-v4 candidates are checked after v5 is added
- **THEN** each historical candidate reproduces its committed source and generated identities byte-for-byte

#### Scenario: V5 carries positive and negative behavior
- **WHEN** v5 is tested in clean supported clients
- **THEN** its evidence accepts stable preference, recurring routine, historical baseline, durable affiliation, exact adopted-artifact, stable recurring-identity, existing-Entity hydration, and reviewed curation cases
- **AND** rejects the paired fleeting, one-off, incidental, trivial, tentative, unselected-draft, wrong-variant, false-save, ambiguous-identity auto-selection, duplicate-Entity, unconfirmed-curation, and false-terminal cases

#### Scenario: Fixture drift invalidates v5
- **WHEN** the v5 core skill or candidate-scoped behavior fixture changes
- **THEN** v5 compatibility, package, lock, archive, and promotion verification fail until fresh evidence is produced

#### Scenario: Promotion requires bound client evidence
- **WHEN** v5 is considered for promotion
- **THEN** promotion refuses a wrong profile, stale package, combined-fixture-digest mismatch, or missing positive and negative clean-client traces from any of the four behavior families

#### Scenario: Promoted v5 is never rolled back by mutation
- **WHEN** a correction is discovered after v5 promotion
- **THEN** v5 source, fixture, package, lock, archive, and promotion identities remain unchanged
- **AND** the correction is released through v6 while selection may move away from v5
