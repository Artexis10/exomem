## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shared Hosted behavior ships through one immutable v5 candidate
This change SHALL own the single `hosted-alpha-agent-v5` release for changed baseline and generated-artifact adoption behavior and SHALL NOT alter the source bytes, generated bytes, locks, archives, definitions, fixtures, or promotion records of v1-v4. V5 SHALL own every skill it ships, including its core skill, and SHALL bind the combined baseline and artifact behavior-fixture digest into its compatibility, package, lock, archive, and promotion evidence. No sibling change SHALL independently render, lock, archive, promote, or roll back another v5 candidate.

#### Scenario: Historical candidates reproduce unchanged
- **WHEN** v1-v4 candidates are checked after v5 is added
- **THEN** each historical candidate reproduces its committed source and generated identities byte-for-byte

#### Scenario: V5 carries positive and negative behavior
- **WHEN** v5 is tested in clean supported clients
- **THEN** its evidence accepts stable preference, recurring routine, historical baseline, durable affiliation, and exact adopted-artifact cases
- **AND** rejects the paired fleeting, one-off, incidental, trivial, tentative, unselected-draft, wrong-variant, and false-save cases

#### Scenario: Fixture drift invalidates v5
- **WHEN** the v5 core skill or candidate-scoped behavior fixture changes
- **THEN** v5 compatibility, lock, archive, and promotion verification fail until fresh evidence is produced

#### Scenario: Promotion requires bound client evidence
- **WHEN** v5 is considered for promotion
- **THEN** promotion refuses a wrong profile, stale package, combined-fixture-digest mismatch, or missing baseline or artifact positive and negative clean-client traces

#### Scenario: Promoted v5 is never rolled back by mutation
- **WHEN** a correction is discovered after v5 promotion
- **THEN** v5 source, fixture, package, lock, archive, and promotion identities remain unchanged
- **AND** the correction is released through v6 while selection may move away from v5
