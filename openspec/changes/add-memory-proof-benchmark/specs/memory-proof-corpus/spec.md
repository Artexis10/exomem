## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Deterministic Seeded Corpus Generation
The corpus generator SHALL derive every generated artifact from an explicit
master seed with per-template child seeds, so that the same seed regenerates a
byte-identical text corpus and logically identical binary artifacts on a clean
checkout, and adding or editing one template MUST NOT change any other
template's generated bytes. Each generated corpus SHALL carry a manifest
recording the generator version, seed, template/variant inventory, renderer
library versions, degradations, and per-artifact content hashes (a logical
content hash as primary identity and a byte hash as secondary).

#### Scenario: Same seed regenerates identically
- **WHEN** the generator runs twice with the same master seed on the same
  checkout
- **THEN** the two corpus manifests are identical, every text artifact is
  byte-identical, and every binary artifact has an identical logical hash

#### Scenario: Template isolation
- **WHEN** a new scenario template is registered and generation reruns with an
  unchanged master seed
- **THEN** the artifacts of every pre-existing template are unchanged

### Requirement: Bitemporal Ground-Truth Oracle
The corpus SHALL model claims with explicit dual-axis status spans — world
validity (valid-from/valid-to) and knowledge time (recorded-at) — over the
states current, superseded, partially superseded, revoked, disputed,
tentative, confirmed, disproved, and unknown, each span justified by a source
assertion. A pure oracle SHALL be the single source of truth for current
truth, as-of truth, change narratives, required citations (including
transitive expansion to original sources), and audience-allowed views; the
generator MUST derive every expected answer from the oracle and MUST NOT
hand-write expected records. Generation SHALL fail on a status span that no
assertion justifies.

#### Scenario: As-of answer before and after a reversal
- **WHEN** a decision is reversed in week 6 and queries ask for the decision
  as of week 4 and as of week 8
- **THEN** the oracle-derived expected answers differ accordingly, and the
  week-4 expectation cites the pre-reversal source while the week-8
  expectation cites the reversal

#### Scenario: Unjustified span rejected
- **WHEN** a template authors a superseded status span without a superseding
  assertion
- **THEN** generation fails naming the claim and span rather than emitting a
  corpus

### Requirement: Scenario Family Coverage
The generated suite SHALL cover seven scenario families — temporal/evolving
truth, epistemics/provenance, corpus maintenance/entropy over a simulated
12-week ingestion schedule, identity/graph, multimodal/numeric evidence,
governance/adversarial content, and query behaviour (including unanswerable,
clarification-required, and must-not-activate queries) — with at least 16
distinct scenario templates, at least 4 deterministic variants per template
where variation is meaningful, and at least 200 evaluated questions overall,
spanning domain slices that map to the shipped knowledge packs. Each query
SHALL predeclare its expected answer, required and forbidden claims, required
citations, forbidden disclosures, abstention/clarification flags, expected
uncertainty behaviour, and whether memory should activate.

#### Scenario: Suite meets declared minimums
- **WHEN** a release corpus is generated
- **THEN** its manifest reports at least 16 templates, the declared variant
  counts, and at least 200 questions, each with a complete oracle-derived
  expected record

#### Scenario: Adversarial content is data
- **WHEN** a source artifact contains prompt-injection-shaped instructions or
  misleading metadata
- **THEN** the corpus marks it adversarial and the expected records score
  products on treating it as untrusted data, never on obeying it

### Requirement: Privacy-Safe Synthetic Content
Corpus content SHALL be synthesized from committed syllable/grammar wordbanks
containing no real personal names, private data, or absolute local paths;
generated corpora and run outputs SHALL NOT be committed to the repository.
The public identity of a corpus release SHALL be its committed release
manifest plus the committed generator, and the repository privacy scan SHALL
pass over the wordbank and over a freshly generated corpus.

#### Scenario: Privacy scan of generated content
- **WHEN** the repository privacy scanner runs over the wordbank module and a
  freshly generated corpus tree
- **THEN** it reports zero findings

#### Scenario: Corpus identity without committed binaries
- **WHEN** a corpus release is published
- **THEN** only its text manifest (hashes, seed, versions, degradations) is
  committed and regeneration from the same seed reproduces matching hashes

### Requirement: Native Renderers With Parity Reports
The corpus SHALL be product-neutral, and per-product native renderers SHALL
express corpus facts in each product's documented public grammar, emitting a
per-fact parity report classifying every fact as represented, degraded, or
unsupported with a reason. A renderer MUST NOT silently drop a fact, and the
neutral corpus MUST NOT encode any single product's ontology or vocabulary
(enforced by a banned-vocabulary lint derived from Exomem's operation, type,
and relation names).

#### Scenario: Fact coverage is complete
- **WHEN** a native renderer finishes rendering a corpus
- **THEN** every fact identifier appears exactly once in its parity report,
  and unsupported facts carry reasons

#### Scenario: Ontology lint
- **WHEN** a template's generated neutral text contains a banned
  product-vocabulary token
- **THEN** the lint fails generation naming the template and token

### Requirement: Sentinel Citation Identity
Every source artifact SHALL embed a visible, deterministic sentinel token that
survives product ingestion as text, and generated source basenames SHALL be
globally unique within a corpus, so citation identity can be scored
deterministically across products without depending on any product's internal
identifiers.

#### Scenario: Sentinel survives ingestion
- **WHEN** the exomem adapter ingests a source and the same source is later
  retrieved
- **THEN** the retrieved content still contains the sentinel token

#### Scenario: Unique basenames
- **WHEN** a corpus is generated
- **THEN** no two source artifacts share a basename

### Requirement: Multimodal Artifact Degradation Is Recorded
Artifact renderers SHALL produce markdown, tabular, and image artifacts with
no dependencies beyond the standard library and Pillow; PDF artifacts SHALL be
produced only when the optional PDF library is importable, and otherwise the
same logical content SHALL be emitted as markdown explicitly marked
pdf-unavailable, with the degradation recorded in the corpus manifest and in
every affected parity report.

#### Scenario: PDF library absent
- **WHEN** the PDF renderer's dependency is not importable during generation
- **THEN** generation still succeeds, the affected artifacts are marked
  pdf-unavailable, and the manifest lists the degradation
