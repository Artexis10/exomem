# Licence change at the Evermatter release

From release LAST_AGPL_RELEASE_NEXT onward, Evermatter's server, CLI and images are licensed under the [Functional Source License, Version 1.1, ALv2 Future License](../../LICENSE) (`FSL-1.1-ALv2`). Earlier releases keep the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later.

## What stays the same

- Self-hosting stays free. You may run, read, modify and redistribute the code for any purpose other than a competing commercial product or service, including internal use in a company.
- Every line stays public.
- The client plugins under `plugins/` and the starter skill under `src/exomem/_scaffold/` stay Apache-2.0.

## What changes

- You may not offer the software, or a service built on it, as a commercial product that competes with it. The licence text defines "Competing Use" exactly.
- Two years after we publish each release, that release also becomes available under the Apache License, Version 2.0, automatically and irrevocably.

## Releases before the change

The last release under AGPL-3.0-or-later is LAST_AGPL_RELEASE, from commit LAST_AGPL_COMMIT. Anyone who holds that release, or any earlier one, keeps every AGPL right to it. The AGPL text is kept in [prior-licence-AGPL-3.0.txt](prior-licence-AGPL-3.0.txt).

## Contributors

Each public release that includes a contribution becomes available under an OSI-approved licence no later than two years after its first publication, as section 3 of the [Contributor Licence Agreement](../../CLA.md) promises. The Apache-2.0 conversion above keeps that promise.
