# Batching photos by theme saves editing time

## Claim

Sorting a trip's photos into themes before any editing starts cuts the total editing time, because one set of adjustments can be reused across a whole theme.

## Why it holds

Photos of one theme share light and colour, so a single preset and one round of tweaks fits most of them. Editing each photo from scratch repeats the same decisions.

## Where it applies

Every trip edit, and the monthly roundup of favourite shots.

## Counter-considerations

A theme with mixed lighting needs a second pass, which gives back some of the time saved.
