# Printing at a local shop beats ordering online

## Claim

Prints from the local shop match the screen better than prints ordered online, because the shop lets me check a test strip before the full run.

## Why it holds

A test strip exposes colour drift early. Online orders print the whole batch with no preview.

## Where it applies

Prints for the wall and for gifts.

## Counter-considerations

The shop costs more per print and closes early on weekends.
