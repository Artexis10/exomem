# Weekly pantry check

## Problem

Duplicate purchases and expired staples.

## Solution

1. Photograph the pantry shelves on Friday morning.
2. Note anything that expires within two weeks.
3. Move those items to the front of the shelf.

## When to use

Every week before the grocery order is drafted.

## When NOT to use

During a holiday week when the pantry is closed.
