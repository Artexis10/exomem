# Sunday meal-prep routine

## Problem

Weeknight dinners fall apart when nothing is prepared ahead.

## Solution

1. Check the pantry list and cross off what is in stock.
2. Draft a menu of four dinners for the week.
3. Order the groceries on Saturday afternoon, for Sunday delivery.
4. Batch-cook one grain and one sauce on Sunday.
5. Portion and label the containers for the week.

## When to use

Any week with at least four dinners to cover at home.

## When NOT to use

Weeks with travel of more than two nights.
