# Herb planter options

## Question

Which planter suits a south-facing balcony herb garden with a 60 cm rail?

## Findings

- A cedar box fits the rail and keeps roots cooler than metal in afternoon sun.
- A narrow metal trough is cheaper but heats up by early afternoon.
- A hanging basket frees the floor but dries out within a day in summer.
