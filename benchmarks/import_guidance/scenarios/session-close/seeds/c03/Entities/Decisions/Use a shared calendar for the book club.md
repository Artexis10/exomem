# Use a shared calendar for the book club

## Context

Meeting dates were scattered across chat messages and people missed sessions.

## Decision

The club keeps one shared calendar for all meeting dates. Each meeting gets a calendar entry, and reminders go out by email two days before.

## Alternatives considered

Pinning the dates in the group chat. This was rejected because pinned messages get buried.

## Consequences

Every member needs access to the calendar. Dates are changed in one place only.
