# Supplier dispute reply

Follow these steps in order.

1. Open with the invoice number and its date, exactly as the supplier wrote them.
2. State the rate agreed in the purchase order, and name the purchase order number.
3. Offer one call slot on a weekday morning. Offer only one.
4. Do not admit liability, and do not promise a payment date.
5. Sign off as "Accounts team".
