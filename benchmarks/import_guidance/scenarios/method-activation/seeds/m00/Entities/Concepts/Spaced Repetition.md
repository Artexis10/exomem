# Spaced Repetition

## Summary

A study technique that shows each card again just before it would be forgotten. Correct answers push the next review further out, and mistakes bring it closer.

## Why in the KB

It is the engine behind the daily language review, and it applies to any fact worth keeping.

## Practical notes

- Intervals grow roughly by a factor of two after each correct answer.
- Cards should test one fact each.
- A missed week is easier to catch up on if new cards are paused.
