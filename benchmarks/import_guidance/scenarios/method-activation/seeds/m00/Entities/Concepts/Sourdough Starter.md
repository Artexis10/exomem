# Sourdough Starter

## Summary

A living culture of flour, water, wild yeast and bacteria, kept on the kitchen counter and fed once a day in warm weather, once a week in the fridge.

## Why in the KB

Every bake depends on its strength. The hydration trials assume a starter that doubles within six hours of feeding.

## Care notes

- Feed at a one to five to five ratio of starter, flour and water.
- A sour smell of acetone means it is hungry, not spoiled.
- Discard goes into pancakes.
