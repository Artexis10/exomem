# Companion Planting

## Summary

Growing certain plants next to each other so that one helps the other, for example by attracting pollinators, repelling pests or fixing nitrogen in the soil.

## Why in the KB

The raised bed layout uses a few of these pairings, and the planting calendar relies on them.

## Pairings in use

- Marigolds beside tomatoes to discourage whitefly.
- Beans with sweetcorn so the corn gives the beans something to climb.
- Chives among the carrots to confuse the carrot fly.
