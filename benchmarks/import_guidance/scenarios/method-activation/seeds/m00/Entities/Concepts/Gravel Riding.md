# Gravel Riding

## Summary

Cycling on unpaved roads and tracks with a drop-bar bike that has wide tyres. It sits between road riding and mountain biking.

## Why in the KB

It anchors the tyre pressure notes, the maintenance routines and the route ideas from the club.

## What matters in practice

- Tyres matter more than the frame: width and pressure decide the comfort.
- Gear range matters on steep loose climbs, where a small front ring helps.
- Rides take longer than the distance suggests, so plan on twelve kilometres an hour on rough tracks.
