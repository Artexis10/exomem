# Harmonic Progressions

## Summary

A sequence of harmonies that supports a melody. A few common progressions cover a large share of popular songs and make good practice material.

## Why in the KB

The practice routine uses them to learn harmony changes and rhythm together.

## Progressions in the book

- One, four, five in a major key, the basic blues shape.
- One, five, six minor, four, the pop favourite.
- Two, five, one, the jazz cadence.

- relates_to [[Notes/Procedures/guitar-practice-routine]]

## Practice use

Each progression gets played in three keys per week, with a slow strum and then a picked pattern.
