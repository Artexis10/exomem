# Exposure Triangle

## Summary

The three settings that decide how bright a photograph is: aperture, shutter speed and ISO. Changing one needs a change in another to keep the same brightness.

## Why in the KB

The night sky checklist and the lens notes assume the trade-offs between these settings.

## Trade-offs in short

- A wider aperture lets in more light and shrinks the sharp zone.
- A slower shutter lets in more light and blurs motion.
- A higher ISO brightens the frame and adds grain.
