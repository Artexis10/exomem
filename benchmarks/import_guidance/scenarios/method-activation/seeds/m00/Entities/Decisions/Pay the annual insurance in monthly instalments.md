# Pay the annual insurance in monthly instalments

## Context

The home insurance renews as one large payment in spring, which strained the current account.

## Decision

Pay the premium in twelve monthly instalments by direct debit.

## Alternatives considered

Keep paying annually from a dedicated savings pot. Rejected because the interest earned did not cover the extra monthly charge by much, and the pot was easy to raid.

## Consequences

The total cost is slightly higher, and the spring cash pressure is gone.
