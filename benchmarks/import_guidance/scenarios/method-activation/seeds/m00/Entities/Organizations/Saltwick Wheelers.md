# Saltwick Wheelers

## Summary

A friendly cycling club that meets on Sunday mornings and runs a monthly gravel route. Membership is informal and the rides split into two speed groups.

## Why in the KB

The club supplies most of the route ideas and the repair clinic dates.

## Practical notes

- Rides leave from the harbour car park at 09:00.
- The slower group stops at the beach café.
- A repair clinic runs on the first Saturday of the month.
