# Tern Island Ferry Co

## Summary

A small operator that runs the passenger ferry between the mainland harbour and Saltwick on the Marrow Coast. It carries bicycles for a fee and sells tickets online and at the quay.

## Why in the KB

The ferry timetable decides the shape of any weekend on the coast.

## Practical notes

- The Friday morning crossing sells out first.
- Changes to a booking are free up to 48 hours before sailing.
- The quay office closes an hour before the last sailing.
