# Corbel Hardware

## Summary

The independent hardware shop at the end of the high street. It cuts keys, mixes paint to order and keeps loose washers and fixings in drawers.

## Why in the KB

It is the first stop for repair parts and paint, and the staff know the older plumbing fittings.

## Practical notes

- Closed on Sundays, open late on Thursdays.
- They match paint from a small chip of the old colour.
- Bring the broken part rather than guessing the size.
