# Brindle Credit Union

## Summary

The member-owned credit union that holds the savings accounts and the emergency fund. It pays a modest interest rate and has no monthly fees.

## Why in the KB

The savings notes and the insurance decision both refer to it.

## Practical notes

- Transfers between accounts arrive the same day.
- The mobile app exports statements as CSV, which Pebble Ledger can import.
- Branch hours are short, so most business happens online.
