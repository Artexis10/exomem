# Marrow Coast weekend

## Question

How do we spend a long weekend on the Marrow Coast with one day of cycling and no car?

## Findings

- The Tern Island ferry leaves at 08:40 on Fridays and returns at 18:10 on Sundays. Bikes travel for a small fee if booked.
- Saltwick has two guesthouses within walking distance of the harbour. One is closed on Sundays.
- The cliff path to Quillmere is about 32 km round trip and mostly gravel.
- Evening meals in Saltwick need booking a day ahead in the shoulder season.
