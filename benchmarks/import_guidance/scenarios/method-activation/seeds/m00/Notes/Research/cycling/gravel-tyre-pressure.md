# Gravel tyre pressure

## Question

What pressure suits 40 mm tubeless tyres on mixed gravel and road for a 75 kg rider?

## Findings

- Around 38 psi front and 42 psi rear feels fast on tarmac and still absorbs gravel chatter.
- Dropping to 32 psi on loose descents improved grip but made the front feel vague.
- Pressure should be checked before every ride, since tubeless tyres seep air.
