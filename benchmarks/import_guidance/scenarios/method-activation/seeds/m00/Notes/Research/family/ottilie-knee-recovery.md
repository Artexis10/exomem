# Ottilie's knee recovery

## Question

What can we do to make Ottilie's recovery after her knee operation easier at family gatherings?

## Findings

- Her physiotherapist wants short walks every day and no long drives for the first three months.
- A firm chair with arms is easier for her than the sofa.
- She tires in the afternoon, so lunch should be the main meal when she visits.
