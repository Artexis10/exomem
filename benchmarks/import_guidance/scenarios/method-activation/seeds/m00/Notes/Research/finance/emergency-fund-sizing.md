# Emergency fund sizing

## Question

How many months of expenses should sit in the emergency fund for a household with one steady income and one irregular one?

## Findings

- Four months of essential spending covers most single-event shocks.
- The irregular income justifies two extra months, because its gaps last longer than a missed paycheck.
- The fund should stay in an instant-access account that pays at least a little interest.
