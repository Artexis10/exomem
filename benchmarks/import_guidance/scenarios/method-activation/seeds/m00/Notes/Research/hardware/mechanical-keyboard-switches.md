# Mechanical keyboard switches

## Question

Which switch type suits long typing sessions in a shared flat?

## Findings

- Linear switches feel smooth and are quiet when bottoming out is soft.
- Tactile switches give feedback at the actuation point and tire the fingers less over a day.
- Clicky switches are out. The flat is too small.
- Lighter springs reduce fatigue but increase typos.

The tactile mid-weight option was the pick.
