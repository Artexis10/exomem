# Hallway repaint options

## Question

Which paint and finish survive a narrow hallway with bikes, bags and muddy boots?

## Findings

- A scrubbable matt finish hides wall flaws and wipes clean with a damp cloth.
- Eggshell is more durable at the bottom third of the wall but shows every dent in the plaster.
- Two coats over a stain-blocking primer cover the old scuffs better than three coats of paint alone.
- A pale warm grey makes the hallway feel wider than the current dark green.
