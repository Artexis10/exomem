# A Field Guide to Small Systems

## Question

What does this book about how small systems fail teach that applies to everyday projects?

## Findings

- Most failures start at a hand-off between two parts, not inside one part.
- Feedback loops that are slow are more dangerous than ones that are noisy.
- A system with no slack cannot absorb a small mistake, so every plan needs one deliberately idle margin.
- The chapter on checklists is the most practical and the shortest.

## Open threads

- Try the slack idea on the weekly schedule.
