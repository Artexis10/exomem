# Raised bed layout

## Question

How should the four raised beds on the allotment be arranged for easy access and good light?

## Findings

- Beds one metre wide can be reached from both sides without stepping on the soil.
- Running the beds north to south gives both sides an even share of sun.
- Paths of 60 cm are wide enough for a wheelbarrow if the corners are rounded.
- Tall crops such as beans go on the north end so they do not shade the lettuce.
