# Bathroom sealant peeled after a month

## What happened

New sealant around the bath lifted at one corner after four weeks and let water under the edge.

## Cause

The old sealant was removed but the joint was not fully dry and degreased, and the bath was filled again too soon.

## Lesson

Let the joint dry for a full day, wipe it with spirit cleaner, tape both sides and leave the sealant to cure for 24 hours before any water goes near it.
