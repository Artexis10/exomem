# Sourdough hydration trials

## Hypothesis

A wetter dough gives a more open crumb without making the loaf hard to shape.

## Setup

Same flour, same starter, same oven. Only the water changes between bakes. The baseline is 72 percent hydration. The first trial bakes at 75 percent.

## Results so far

- 72 percent: reliable, tight even crumb, good for sandwiches.
- 75 percent: slightly more open, still easy to shape.

## Next

Try 78 percent on the weekend loaf and compare the crumb and the way the loaf holds its shape on the tray.
