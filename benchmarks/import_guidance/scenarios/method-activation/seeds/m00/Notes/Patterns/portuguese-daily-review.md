# Portuguese daily review

## Problem

New words fade within days unless they are revisited.

## Solution

1. Open the flashcard deck and clear the due cards first, ten minutes at most.
2. Add at most five new words a day, each with an example sentence from the day's reading.
3. Say three sentences aloud using words from today's cards.
4. Note one mistake from a conversation lesson in the mistakes list.

## When to use

Every morning before work.

## When NOT to use

On days with a conversation lesson, which replaces the aloud step.
