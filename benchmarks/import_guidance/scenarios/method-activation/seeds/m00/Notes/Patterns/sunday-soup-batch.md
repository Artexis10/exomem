# Sunday soup batch

## Problem

Lunches at the desk drift into takeaway when nothing is ready on Monday.

## Solution

1. Pick one soup that freezes well and buy for a double batch.
2. Sweat the onions slowly while the stock warms.
3. Cook, cool quickly in shallow trays, and portion into six containers.
4. Freeze four portions and keep two in the fridge.

## When to use

Any week without a lunch meeting outside the office.

## When NOT to use

Weeks when the freezer is already full.
