# Water in the morning, not the evening

## Claim

Watering the allotment early in the morning keeps plants healthier than watering at dusk.

## Why it holds

Leaves dry in the day sun, which cuts fungal disease and slug activity. Evening water sits on the soil overnight and invites both.

## Where it applies

Beds of tomatoes, beans and lettuce in summer.

## Counter-considerations

On very hot days a second light watering at dusk helps seedlings, applied at the roots only.

## Evidence

Two summers of side-by-side beds showed less mildew and fewer slug holes on the morning-watered bed.
