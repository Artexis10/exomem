# A prime lens forces better framing

## Claim

Shooting a day with only a fixed focal length produces better compositions than the same day with a zoom.

## Why it holds

Without a zoom ring the photographer must move, and moving reveals angles that a lazy zoom never finds.

## Where it applies

Street walks and travel days when time allows.

## Counter-considerations

Fast-moving events need the flexibility of a zoom.

## Evidence

The best frames from the last two city weekends all came from the days with the 35 mm lens only. The zoom days produced more shots and fewer keepers.
