# Automatic transfers beat willpower

## Claim

A standing order that moves money to savings on payday saves more than a promise to save what is left over.

## Why it holds

What is left over is usually nothing. A transfer that happens before the money is seen requires no decision each month.

## Where it applies

The emergency fund and the holiday pot.

## Counter-considerations

Set the amount conservatively so the current account never goes overdrawn.

## Evidence

The holiday pot grew steadily in the months with a standing order and stalled in the months when the transfer was done by hand.
