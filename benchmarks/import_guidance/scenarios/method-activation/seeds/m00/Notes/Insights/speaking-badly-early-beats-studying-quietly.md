# Speaking badly early beats studying quietly

## Claim

Starting conversation practice in the first month teaches more than waiting until the grammar feels solid.

## Why it holds

Mistakes in speech are immediate and specific, and a tutor corrects the ones that matter. Quiet study corrects nothing until the mistake is already habit.

## Where it applies

The Portuguese lessons and any other spoken language.

## Counter-considerations

Pure beginners need a small stock of words first, or the lesson turns into pointing at things.
