# Slow practice fixes fast mistakes

## Claim

A passage that goes wrong at speed is repaired faster by playing it at half tempo until it is clean than by repeating it fast.

## Why it holds

At speed the hands rehearse the mistake. At half tempo the attention catches the exact note where the error starts.

## Where it applies

Every hard bar in the current piece.

## Counter-considerations

Slow practice should be followed by a few passes near full speed, or the fix does not carry over.
