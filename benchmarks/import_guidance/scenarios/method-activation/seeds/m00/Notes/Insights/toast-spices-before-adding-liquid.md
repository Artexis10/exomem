# Toast spices before adding liquid

## Claim

Dry-toasting whole spices for a minute before any liquid goes in gives a deeper flavour than adding them straight to the pot.

## Why it holds

Heat releases the aromatic oils, and fat or water afterwards carries them through the dish. Spices dropped into cold liquid taste flatter.

## Where it applies

Curries, soups and stews that start with whole seeds.

## Counter-considerations

Ground spices burn in seconds and are better added after the onions.
