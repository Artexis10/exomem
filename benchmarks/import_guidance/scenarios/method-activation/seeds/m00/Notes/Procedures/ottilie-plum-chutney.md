# Ottilie's plum chutney

## Scope

About four jars. It keeps for a year in a cool cupboard.

## Steps

1. Stone and chop a kilo of dark plums.
2. Simmer them with two chopped onions, 300 g of brown sugar and 400 ml of cider vinegar.
3. Add a thumb of grated ginger, a teaspoon of mustard seed and a pinch of chilli.
4. Cook slowly for about an hour, until a spoon leaves a trail.
5. Pour into warm sterilised jars and seal at once.

## Notes

She says it is better after six weeks in the jar, and that the onions should be cooked right down.
