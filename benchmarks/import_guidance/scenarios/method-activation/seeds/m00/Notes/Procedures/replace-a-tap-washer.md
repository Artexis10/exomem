# Replace a tap washer

## Scope

For a traditional kitchen tap with a rubber washer. It does not cover ceramic disc taps.

## Steps

1. Turn off the water at the isolation valve under the sink and open the tap to drain it.
2. Remove the cover and unscrew the headgear with a spanner.
3. Take out the old washer and press in a new one of the same size.
4. Smear a little plumber's grease on the threads and reassemble.
5. Turn the water back on and check for drips for a few minutes.

## Notes

Keep the old washer in a pocket when buying a new one. Sizes vary more than expected.
