# Guitar practice routine

## Scope

Twenty minutes on weekdays, thirty at weekends.

## Steps

1. Warm up with five minutes of slow scales, two octaves, with a metronome.
2. Spend ten minutes on the current piece, one hard passage only.
3. Play through one finished piece for fun.
4. Write down the metronome speed reached for the hard passage.

## Notes

Stop when the hand tires. Short and daily beats long and rare.

The hard passage in the current piece moved from sixty to seventy-two beats a minute over three weeks.
