# Chain cleaning and lube

## Scope

For the gravel bike after any wet or dusty ride. It takes ten minutes.

## Steps

1. Shift to the smallest rear sprocket and wipe the chain with a rag.
2. Spray degreaser on a brush and scrub the chain, the sprockets and the jockey wheels.
3. Rinse with a little warm water and dry the chain thoroughly.
4. Add one drop of lube per link, turn the pedals backwards for a minute.
5. Wipe off the excess so dirt does not stick.

## Notes

A clean chain lasts roughly twice as long as a neglected one.
