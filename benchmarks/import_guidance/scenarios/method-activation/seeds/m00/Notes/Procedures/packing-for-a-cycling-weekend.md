# Packing for a cycling weekend

## Scope

Two nights, panniers only, no car support.

## Steps

1. Lay out clothes for one riding day and one evening, and roll them.
2. Pack the puncture kit, a spare tube and the small pump.
3. Take one charger that works for the phone and the bike light.
4. Bring a light waterproof layer in every season.
5. Weigh the panniers. Anything over eight kilos each gets cut.

## Notes

The ferry company checks bike labels at the quay, so write the booking name on the frame tag.
