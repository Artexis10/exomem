# Weeknight lentil curry

## Scope

For two people, on a night with no time. Quantities double cleanly.

## Steps

1. Toast a teaspoon each of cumin and coriander seed in the dry pan.
2. Add a chopped onion and cook until soft, then stir in garlic and ginger.
3. Add a cup of red lentils, three cups of water and a spoonful of tomato paste.
4. Simmer for fifteen minutes until the lentils collapse.
5. Finish with lemon juice and salt.

## Notes

Leftovers improve overnight. Add a splash of water when reheating.
