# Trip planning

How I want a trip put together and kept up to date.

## When it applies

I am arranging a trip, changing one, or confirming a draft. This includes day trips and weekends away.

## When it does not apply

- Pure browsing or daydreaming about places.
- Questions about a technical tool or a hobby that happen to be set in a place.
- Everyday errands.

## Steps

1. Check my notes for an existing note on this trip, and read it first. Update that note instead of starting a new one.
2. Confirm the fixed points: dates, who is going, transport that cannot be changed.
3. Build the itinerary day by day with realistic travel times, including the boring parts such as luggage and queues.
4. List what needs booking, in the order of how little slack each has. Return transport comes before rooms and meals.
5. When I approve a draft, mark it approved in the note, list the bookings still open, and give the next deadline.
6. Add a short packing list based on my packing procedure.
7. Keep the open questions at the bottom of the note.

## Rules

- Do not book anything on my behalf. List what to book and where.
- Record changes of mind as changes to the note, not as a second note.
- Check opening days. Several places close on Sundays.

## Where results go

The trip's research note under Notes/Research/travel, for example [[Notes/Research/travel/marrow-coast-weekend]].

## Format of the note

Keep the trip note in this order: status, fixed points, itinerary by day, bookings with their deadlines, packing, open questions. Mark the status as draft, approved or booked. Keep the whole note under two pages.

## Common mistakes

- Planning every hour. Leave a free morning in each trip.
- Forgetting travel time to and from the station or the quay.
- Booking rooms before the transport that cannot move.
- Letting the draft and the approved version drift apart in two places.
