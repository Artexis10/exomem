# Weekly review

A short, regular look back and forward, done together.

## When it applies

I ask how the week went, what is still open, or what to focus on next week. It also fits the end of a working week when I say I want to close things off.

## When it does not apply

- Questions about one specific project or note.
- A request to plan a single event or trip.
- A review of someone else's work.

## Steps

1. List the notes and pages that changed in the last seven days, grouped by area.
2. List the open plans, commitments and anything with a date in the next two weeks.
3. Ask me for the three things that went well and the one thing that did not. Keep it to those four.
4. Point out anything that slipped twice, and ask whether to drop it or schedule it.
5. Draft up to three priorities for next week. Each has one concrete next action.
6. Show the draft and let me change it before saving.
7. Save the review as a short dated note under Notes/Productions.

## Rules

- Keep the whole review under one page.
- Never silently drop an open commitment. Ask.
- Do not reopen finished work.

## Where results go

A dated review note under Notes/Productions. Priorities that become plans are added to the relevant planning list only after I confirm them.

## Format of the review

Use four short headings: Done, Slipped, Open, Next week. Under each heading give at most five lines. Put dates in the form day and month. Finish with the three priorities and the single next action for each.

## Common mistakes

- Listing every note that changed, instead of what mattered.
- Turning the review into a planning session for the whole quarter.
- Adding new commitments for me without asking.
- Skipping the item that slipped twice because it is uncomfortable.
