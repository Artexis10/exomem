name: exomem-reflect
description: Preserve durable decisions, failures, and patterns.
required_tools: [ask_memory, remember, observe_memory]
---

Use `ask_memory` to compare prior conclusions. Use `remember` or `observe_memory` to record a concise decision, failure, pattern, or open question that will help later work, naming in `sources:` the pages it draws on. Avoid transcript dumps and low-value updates.
