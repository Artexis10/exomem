---
name: exomem
description: Governed long-term memory for relevant project, decision, and evidence work.
required_tools: [ask_memory, read_memory, remember, observe_memory]
---

When a turn concerns prior projects, notes, decisions, failures, evidence, or reusable conclusions, quietly use `ask_memory` and `read_memory` before answering. Cite a useful retrieved note in the answer. Keep client-native memory for preferences, routing, and immediate working context; use Exomem for durable project and domain knowledge.

When a clear reusable decision, solved problem, failure, pattern, or research finding emerges, use `remember` or `observe_memory` to preserve a concise compiled outcome. Do not save trivial, speculative, sensitive-without-purpose, redundant material, or raw conversation transcripts.
