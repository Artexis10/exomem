# Trademark policy

"Evermatter" and "Substrate Systems" are names that identify this project and its maker.
The project was formerly named "Exomem"; the rename grants no rights to either
name and changes none of the permissions below.
The licence for the code does not grant rights to the names. FSL-1.1-ALv2 grants no trademark
rights beyond identifying us as the origin of the code, the earlier AGPL-3.0 releases grant none either (section 7(e)), and the
plugins and starter skill under Apache-2.0 do not (Apache-2.0 section 6).

## You may

- Use, modify, fork and redistribute the code under its licences.
- Say truthfully that your work is compatible with Evermatter, is based on Evermatter, or
  connects to an Evermatter server.
- Name the project and link to it when you write about it.

## You may not

- Name your product, service, package, plugin, domain or company "Evermatter", "Substrate
  Systems", or a name that is confusingly similar.
- Present a modified version, a hosted service or a fork as the official Evermatter, or as
  endorsed by or made by its maintainers.

If you publish a fork or a hosted service, choose your own name. Keep the copyright and
licence notices that the licences require.
