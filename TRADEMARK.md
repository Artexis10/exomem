"Exomem" and "Substrate Systems" are names that identify this project and its maker.
The licence for the code does not grant rights to the names. This is the AGPL-3.0
section 7(e) position: the AGPL does not give you trademark rights, and the plugins and
starter skill under Apache-2.0 do not either (Apache-2.0 section 6).

## You may

- Use, modify, fork and redistribute the code under its licences.
- Say truthfully that your work is compatible with Exomem, is based on Exomem, or
  connects to an Exomem server.
- Name the project and link to it when you write about it.

## You may not

- Name your product, service, package, plugin, domain or company "Exomem", "Substrate
  Systems", or a name that is confusingly similar.
- Present a modified version, a hosted service or a fork as the official Exomem, or as
  endorsed by or made by its maintainers.

If you publish a fork or a hosted service, choose your own name. Keep the copyright and
licence notices that the licences require.
